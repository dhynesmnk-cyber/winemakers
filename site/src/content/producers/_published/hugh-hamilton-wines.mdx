Hugh Hamilton Wines sits in McLaren Vale, South Australia. The producer describes its cellar door as set among the vineyards with a 270 degree view of the region, though the page gives no street address or suburb.

The business describes itself as run by the fifth and sixth generation of the Hamilton family, naming Hugh Hamilton and his daughter Mary Hamilton as the current generation. It states 185 years of family wine history across six generations and calls itself Australia's oldest wine family.

<Pull attribution="Hugh Hamilton Wines, on its home page">
  Hugh Hamilton and his daughter Mary Hamilton are the 5th and 6th generation of the Australian Hamilton wine dynasty that stretches back over 185 years
</Pull>

The record names four varieties: Shiraz, Saperavi, Cabernet Sauvignon and Pinot Gris. Several labelled wines appear on the site, including 'The Villain' Cabernet Sauvignon, 'The Rascal' Shiraz, 'The Trickster' Pinot Gris, 'Pure Black' 2018 and 'Black Ops' Shiraz Saperavi, a blend of the two red varieties that the producer describes as covertly blended. 'Pure Black' 2018 and 'Icon' Shiraz Saperavi appear to refer to the same wine based on the page's wording, though this is not confirmed there.

The 'Black Ops' Shiraz Saperavi 2021 was ranked 91st, at 92 points, in Wine Spectator's Top 100 Wines of 2025, one of two Australian wines on that list according to the site. 'The Oddball' 2023 was named in Matthew Jukes' '100 Best Australian Wines 2026'.

No organic or biodynamic status is stated on the page, and no detail is given on fruit sourcing. The practices the record checks for, wild ferment, unfined, unfiltered and minimal sulphur, are all recorded as not stated.

Cellar door tastings are available, though no hours are published. The site advertises a tasting flight called the Black Sheep, comprising 'The Trickster' Pinot Gris, 'The Rascal' Shiraz, 'The Villain' Cabernet Sauvignon and two flavoured almond products, all in full-size 750ml bottles. A membership programme, the Black Sheep Club, is described as offering access to limited wines and events, though the page gives no further detail on cost or terms.

One priced bundle is stated: the Big Bold Red Trio of 'The Villain' Cabernet Sauvignon, 'Black Ops' Shiraz Saperavi and 'The Rascal' Shiraz, at $99, discounted from $120. The site also states that a wine not to a visitor's taste after the first two sips will be replaced with something else.

No founding year, address, opening hours, certification or production volume are published on the page.
