Baxter Valley Estate is an estate winery in the Mornington Peninsula region of Victoria, established in 1992 by Chris and Maria Tsatsaris. The record does not give a street address or suburb, so this entry records only the state and region.

The vineyard is planted along the front drive with Shiraz, Cabernet and Pinot. A back block carries Cabernet, Merlot, Shiraz, Pinot, Chardonnay and Sauvignon Blanc. All of the fruit used is grown on the property; nothing is bought in. Grapes are hand-picked and the wines are made on site.

One wine on the list is a field blend of Chardonnay and Sauvignon Blanc. It is named "Scooter", after Uncle John, who the record identifies as a family member known by that name. The Shiraz is aged eighteen months in oak, with twenty per cent new American oak, which is the only winemaking detail given for a specific wine.

The producer describes the operation as a family undertaking rather than simply naming its founders:

<Pull attribution="Baxter Valley Estate, on their wines page">
  every step of the process involves our family & friends
</Pull>

Chris and Maria Tsatsaris are named as the founders, and the record's own language ties the day-to-day running of the winery to family and friends, so the entry can fairly call this a family-owned estate.

No organic or biodynamic certification is claimed, and the record does not state that either practice is followed uncertified either. The winery is not recorded as using wild ferment, and none of its wines are described as unfined, unfiltered or made with a minimal-sulphur approach. On what the record gives, this is a conventional estate operation built around its own fruit rather than around a particular technique.

The record contains no tasting hours, no pricing information and no stated cellar door hours. It does state that wines can be purchased at the cellar door and that free local delivery is available on request, so a cellar door evidently operates, even though the hours it keeps are not published. Whether a booking is required to visit is likewise not stated, so this entry does not assume one either way.

The website given for the producer sits on a subdomain of treand.com.au rather than a domain carrying the Baxter Valley Estate name, which is worth noting for anyone trying to confirm the business independently before visiting or ordering.
