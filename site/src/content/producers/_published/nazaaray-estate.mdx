Nazaaray Estate sits on a hillside on the Mornington Peninsula in Victoria, overlooking Greens Bush, part of the Peninsula State forest reserve. The site is exposed to wind from all directions, a detail the producer states outright rather than leaving a reader to guess at the site's exposure.

The estate traces back to 1991, when Paramdeep (Param) Ghumman and his wife Nirmal, a doctor, purchased 50 acres of cattle farmland on the peninsula while still living in Melbourne. The couple had emigrated to Australia from India ten years earlier, in 1981. Param Ghumman is named as the winemaker and Nirmal as co-owner. No other people are named in connection with the business, and independence is recorded as clear, with no parent company, shared address, or shared contact domain found.

A cellar door operates on site and is described as a chance to meet the winemaker and be guided through the latest releases. No opening hours or tasting fees appear in the record, so neither is given here. The winery has also hosted events beyond the cellar door itself: a 2024 Winter Wine Weekend held on 9 and 10 June, and a draw-and-paint day run jointly with the Peninsula Plein Air Painting Group. That painting event was followed by a shared lunch of Indian fusion dishes, a detail that connects back to the Ghummans' own background. The June 9 event is recorded with some specificity: a buffet-style feast of pakoras, lamb rogan josh, vegetable sabzi, daal, sourdough naan, raita, and gulab jamun with poached winter fruit.
