Seville Estate sits in the Upper Yarra Valley, Victoria, about one hour east of Melbourne. The producer describes itself as the first winery established in the Upper Yarra Valley, dating the operation to 1972, the same year its vines went into the ground. Those vines are dry-grown, on volcanic soils, and the vineyard is now more than fifty years old.

Fruit for the current release comes entirely from the estate. The only variety named on the producer's site is Shiraz, specifically a reserve from the 2021 vintage. No other varieties are documented, and the record gives no reason to assume a wider range beyond what is stated.

The cellar door takes walk-in visitors Friday to Sunday, with weekday visits available by appointment. A selection of tasting options is offered, including access to rare and seasonal releases, though no tasting fee is stated on the site. The setting is described as a cellar door set among a native garden. No pricing detail, minimum age, or online shop is published in the record, so none of those are noted here.

Accommodation is available on the property: a homestead that sleeps up to eight, or a separate Vineyard Studio. A restaurant once operated alongside the cellar door; the producer's site currently lists it as on pause rather than closed, which is worth noting as a stated fact rather than a guess about when or whether it will reopen.

There is no ownership statement on the producer's page, no parent company mentioned, and no shared address or contact domain flagged, so independence here is marked for review rather than confirmed one way or the other. The record makes no organic or biodynamic certification claim, and none of wild ferment, unfined, unfiltered, or minimal-intervention practices are noted for this producer.
