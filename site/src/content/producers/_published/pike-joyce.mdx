Pike & Joyce farms a single 25 hectare vineyard at Lenswood in the Adelaide Hills on steep north and east facing slopes of gravelly clay and loam soils. The land was previously under apple and pear orchards. The vineyard is hand pruned and hand picked, and the fruit is chilled overnight in the Joyce family's cool rooms before being transported to Pikes' winery in the Clare Valley for vinification.

The name brings together two families identified on the producer's own site: the Pike family, described as long established in winemaking and viticulture, and the Joyce family, described as fifth generation horticulturists.

The relationship between Pike & Joyce and Pikes, the Clare Valley winery that receives this fruit, is not clarified on the page. Pikes appears to be a separate entity, but no ownership, licensing or contract detail is given, so the connection is stated here only as far as the record supports it.

Ten varieties are planted across the estate: Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Gris, Chardonnay, Gruner Veltliner, Gewurztraminer and Riesling among the whites, and Shiraz, Dolcetto, Nebbiolo and Pinot Noir among the reds. The producer describes its winemaking as combining traditional old world techniques with modern Australian methods, though the record gives no further detail on ferments, vessels or additions, and none of wild ferment, unfined, unfiltered or minimal sulphur handling is claimed as a practice.

No founding year is stated for the vineyard or the label. No organic or biodynamic certification is claimed either.

The cellar door is open seven days a week from 11am to 4pm on a site described as perched atop a hill with 180-degree views overlooking the single estate vineyard. A restaurant on the same site operates daily from midday to 4pm, with a menu the producer describes as focused on local produce and fresh ingredients. Vouchers for the restaurant are available by email, and the property hosts weddings and functions.

No pricing, tasting fee or minimum age is published in the record, and no online shop is mentioned, so none of those details are given here.
