Maxwell Wines sits at McLaren Vale, South Australia, with estate vineyards planted on limestone slopes. The record gives no founding year and no named family or individual behind the operation, so neither is stated here. Independence is marked as needing verification against the ownership register rather than confirmed outright, and no parent company or shared-address signal appears in the Harvester's record.

The winemaking facts on file are general: grapes arrive, are pressed, fermented, and blended, and the resulting wine is matured in barrels. Visitors on the tour are offered a tasting directly from barrel as part of that process. No specific varieties are named anywhere in the record, so none are listed here, and no organic or biodynamic status is claimed.

What the record documents in detail is a single paid visitor experience rather than a walk-in tasting room. It is a behind-the-scenes tour and tasting running two hours, priced at $120 per person, with a minimum of two people required to run it. The tour is hosted by a guide described in the record as an expert Maxwell tour guide, and it is explicitly subject to forecast weather, with rescheduling possible in inclement conditions. The experience includes a private tasting and a barrel tasting, concludes with a premium wine tasting, and offers a mead tasting as an optional add-on. No published open hours appear in the record, which is consistent with a booked, appointment-style visit rather than drop-in access, so cellar door access is recorded here as by appointment.
