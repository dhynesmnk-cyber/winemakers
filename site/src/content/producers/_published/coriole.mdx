Coriole is an estate winery in McLaren Vale, South Australia, founded in 1960 by Hugh and Molly Lloyd. The winery is owned and managed by the Lloyd family today, with Peter Lloyd as General Manager and Duncan Lloyd as Senior Winemaker.

The vineyard sits in the undulating hills of McLaren Vale:

<Pull attribution="Coriole, on their vineyard page">
Situated in the undulating hills of the McLaren Vale wine region, just within sight of the sea
</Pull>

Shiraz is the major variety on the estate, connected to a McLaren Vale tradition spanning over 150 years. The property also grows Cabernet, Grenache and Chenin Blanc. Since 1985, Coriole has worked with Italian varieties. The estate holds the oldest sangiovese and fiano vineyards in Australia, along with Nero d'Avola, Montepulciano and Negroamaro.

All fruit is estate-grown, sourced from the vineyard's own plantings, kitchen garden and farm. No wild ferment, unfined, unfiltered or minimal sulphur dioxide practices are stated. The record does not claim organic or biodynamic status, certified or otherwise.

The Small Ironstone Cellar Door is open seven days a week from 11am to 5pm. Walk-ins are welcome and no booking is required. A tasting fee applies but is waived with purchase. Coriole also runs the Coriole Collective wine club, which sends three mixed-dozen deliveries a year from the Estate Range, the New Australian Collection and the Reserve Range.

A restaurant on site serves food made from local ingredients and produce grown in the property's kitchen garden and farm, open for lunch from Thursday to Monday.

Peter Lloyd grew up among the vineyards and now serves as General Manager. His brother Duncan is the Senior Winemaker. The record describes both as committed to honouring the winery's history while working with alternative varieties and winemaking styles.
