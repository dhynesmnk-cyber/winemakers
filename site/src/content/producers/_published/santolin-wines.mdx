Santolin Wines is a Yarra Valley producer that began in 2012, after its founders had already worked at other wineries in the region. The record states they started with two tonnes of Pinot Noir, one barrel and one tank, a scale small enough to place the operation in the garagiste category rather than among the valley's larger estates.

No address, suburb or cellar door information appears on the source page, and the confidence notes are explicit that no cellar door, tasting room or visitor information is provided. The producer's location in this entry is limited to the region itself, the Yarra Valley, because nothing more specific is on record.

The Harvester's record does not say whether the Pinot Noir and Chardonnay that Santolin makes now comes from an estate vineyard, from purchased fruit, or from both. No vineyard facts are given at all, so fruit source is left out here rather than guessed at.

No parent company, shared address or shared contact domain was detected in the ownership check.

The two wines named in the record have picked up recognition beyond the winery itself. The producer states it won "Best New World Chardonnay" at the Hong Kong International Wine and Spirit Competition and "Best International Chardonnay" at Decanter Asia, along with "Best New Act" at the Young Gun Awards. The record also states that Santolin wines are served in Qantas International Business class. These are the producer's own claims rather than a rating this directory has checked, but they are specific and attributable, naming the competition or the context in each case, and that specificity is why they are repeated here.

What can be said with confidence is the shape of the start: one barrel, one tank, two tonnes of Pinot Noir, and two winemakers building outward from that point in 2012.
