Shaws Road Winery is an estate winery at Arthurs Creek, in the Yarra Valley, north-east of Melbourne. The producer states that the operation continues a family winemaking tradition and describes itself as a family-owned vineyard, though it does not name the family or its members on the site, so the specific ownership structure is not confirmed here.

The vineyard is planted on a hillside site, and the vines are described as more than 40 years old, which qualifies them as old vines by any reasonable measure. The property's own account of its position is specific about what the site looks over:

<Pull attribution="Shaws Road Winery, on its vineyard page">
  Vineyard situated high on a hill with views over the Strathewen valley through to the Great Dividing Ranges at Kinglake
</Pull>

The same material notes that Melbourne, 36 kilometres to the south, is visible from the highest point of the property. Chardonnay and Pinot Noir are the named varieties, and the producer also makes sparkling wine, though no specific sparkling variety is stated.

Winemaking is done on the property rather than elsewhere: the record states that grapes are hand-picked and that wines are crafted onsite by the winery's own winemakers. Fruit is estate grown and, according to the producer, ripened to optimum levels before maturation in small oak barrels, with wines held back and released only after what the site describes as sufficient bottle development. No wild ferment, unfined, unfiltered or minimal-SO2 practices are claimed, and none should be assumed in their absence.

The cellar door is open across the week, though on a limited schedule: Wednesday to Friday from 12pm to 4pm, and Saturday and Sunday from 11:30am to 5pm. Friday evenings run a steak night, and Sunday service includes a roast, both of which point to a restaurant operating on the property rather than a tasting room alone. The site also lists boutique bed and breakfast accommodation on site, an open fire for winter service and air conditioning for warmer months, which together describe a venue built around extended visits rather than a quick tasting.

No pricing, tasting fees or founding date are published in the source material, and none are given here. Independence is not fully resolved by the page itself: the family-ownership language is asserted but unsupported by named individuals or an ABN, and confirming it sits outside what this record can establish.
