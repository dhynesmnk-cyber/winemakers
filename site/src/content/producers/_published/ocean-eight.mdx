Ocean Eight is an estate winery at Red Hill, on the Mornington Peninsula. The label was created in 2004 after Chris and Gail Aylward's family sold their earlier brand, Kooyong, to Port Phillip Estate. Their son, Mike Aylward, who had worked part-time at Kooyong while studying Cell Biology and Genetics at Melbourne University, returned to manage the family's original Red Hill vineyard and formed Ocean Eight from what remained of the family's plantings.

The family bought the 50-acre Red Hill property in 1995, when three acres of vines were already in the ground. Chris and Gail engaged soil scientists and the viticulturist Sandro Mosele to identify the site's terroir before planting further. The Red Hill vineyard sits on red volcanic clay, and the record notes the site as too cold for Pinot Noir, which is why the property is now planted to Pinot Gris. Two further vineyards have joined the portfolio since: a 100-acre property between Merricks and Moorooduc, with 70 acres planted on sandy loam and used for Pinot Noir and Chardonnay, and a 27-acre vineyard at Tuerong, also on sandy loam, where the winery's sparkling wine is produced. The record does not give a variety breakdown for the sparkling wine, nor a date for when the Tuerong vineyard was added.

Luke O'Connor, a former colleague from Mike Aylward's time at Kooyong, is named as Head Viticulturist. Grant van Every, a Melbourne sommelier, is recorded as a mentor to Mike Aylward. The winemaking approach is described in the source as small-batch, with a close approach to vineyard management. Pinot Gris production is stated to have grown from 200 dozen to 3,000 cases, though that figure applies to the Pinot Gris alone rather than to total output, and the record gives no overall production figure for the winery.

Fruit is drawn entirely from the three estate vineyards. The determinations attached to the record show no organic or biodynamic certification, and none of wild ferment, unfined, unfiltered or minimal SO2 is claimed.

The cellar door is open Friday and Saturday from midday to 5pm and Sunday from midday to 3pm. No address, pricing or online shop is given in the record, and no minimum age or logistics detail is stated.

The Mornington Peninsula is described in the source material as a region that has grown in standing over the past fifty years, having earlier been known for apple and cherry orchards rather than vineyards. Ocean Eight's own history runs through an earlier brand: Kooyong's first wines were made in 1999, and its Chardonnay was named Best Chardonnay in Australia by the critic Huon Hooke before the brand was sold in 2004, an award that belongs to Kooyong rather than to Ocean Eight itself.
