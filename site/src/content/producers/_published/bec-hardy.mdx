Bec Hardy is an estate winery based in McLaren Vale, South Australia. The producer's site describes two labels operating from the one cellar door: the contemporary Bec Hardy range and the established Pertaringa brand. No founding year is stated for either.

The producer describes itself as a family-owned winery and states that Bec Hardy is the sixth generation and first female vigneron from the Hardy family, named on the site as one of Australia's oldest wine families.

The cellar door offers tastings both indoors and outdoors, with views across the vineyard, and the record notes it has been recently refurbished. No specific hours are published. No standard tasting fee is stated either; the record notes only that the fee is waived for Wine Club members who purchase six bottles, and that Wine Club membership also includes complimentary tastings at the cellar door. Beyond tastings, Wine Club membership carries a 30% discount off RRP on premium wines and complimentary shipping on all orders, which points to an online ordering arrangement even though no separate shop page is named in the record.

Food is served through The Kitchen, described as occupying a converted winemaking shed that has kept its heritage oak vats. The seasonal menu is built around Fleurieu Peninsula produce and designed for sharing and wine pairing, rather than as a straightforward restaurant offering. The producer also states that it hosts garden tours, premium picnics, and intimate weddings, though no vineyard tour is named specifically among these.
