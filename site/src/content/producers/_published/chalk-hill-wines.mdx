Chalk Hill Wines is an estate winery in McLaren Vale, South Australia, trading since 1964. The producer states that the business has been run by the Harvey family for six generations, though the record does not name a current owner or legal entity, so the directory records this as a producer statement rather than a verified fact of ownership.

<Pull attribution="Chalk Hill Wines, on its site">
Six generations and 185 years later, the Harvey family continue their grape growing and winemaking heritage in McLaren Vale with Chalk Hill
</Pull>

The estate is described on the site as spanning six vineyards across McLaren Vale, and the recorded fruit source is entirely estate-grown, with no purchased fruit mentioned. No specific varieties are listed on the page.

Two labels are named. Wines are released under the Chalk Hill name, and a subset is separately promoted to a second label, Alpha Crucis, once the site says the grapes or finished wine meet a consistent standard of excellence. The record does not state how that assessment is made or by whom.

<Pull attribution="Chalk Hill Wines, on its site">
Rooted in six generations of the Harvey family's devotion to viticulture
</Pull>

The history given on the site reaches back further than the winery's own founding. John Harvey is recorded as arriving in Adelaide in 1839, when the city held around 6,000 people, and as the second person to plant grapevines north of Adelaide, ahead of the founding of the Barossa and Clare Valley wine regions. Chalk Hill itself is dated to 1964, and the record does not explain how the earlier history connects to that founding year.

Tastings are held daily from 11am to 4pm, in either the Chalk Hill Cellar Door or the Alpha Crucis Lounge. A deck is also available after the tasting, according to the site's cellar door information. No tasting fee is stated on the page, and no online shop or shipping details appear in the record.
