Bekkers Wine is based in McLaren Vale, South Australia. The record names two people behind the operation: Toby Bekkers, described as viticulturist, and Emmanuelle Bekkers, described as French-born and as Toby's wife, who is the winemaker. No founding year is stated in the source material, so none is given here.

The record does not publish an address, a vineyard size, or a list of varieties grown or used, and it contains no statement on fruit sourcing. It is also silent on organic or biodynamic farming, wild ferment, fining, filtration, or sulphur use, so none of these is asserted in this entry. Category is recorded as estate winery, a determination the Harvester made from context: a viticulturist and winemaker positioned as the operators, the McLaren Vale address, and a tasting room, though the source page does not state the category outright.

A tasting room is part of the operation. The source material describes it in terms that read as the producer's own description of the space:

The same material describes the room as serving wine in high quality glassware with views. No hours are published in the record, so this entry cannot state whether a visit requires booking ahead. Anyone planning to call in should check directly with the winery.

Most of what Bekkers Wine produces is not sold through open retail. The record states that the majority of production is reserved for an allocation list, and that a membership programme called Bekkers 1er offers priority access and automatic delivery on release. No pricing is published, no shop link is given, and there is no statement on national shipping, so this entry does not claim an online purchase path.
