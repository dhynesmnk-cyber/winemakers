Punt Road Wines farms a 58-hectare vineyard in Coldstream, in the Yarra Valley, on land that the producer states was first planted with vines in 1860 by the pioneering vigneron Hubert de Castella. The cuttings used are recorded as having come from the Airlie Bank vineyard, which ran along Melbourne's Punt Road, and that name now belongs to the property's second wine range alongside the estate's own Punt Road label.

Ownership sits with the Napoleone family, who the producer describes as farming and owning the land across the third and fourth generations. No individual family members are named in the record, so this entry reports the family's role as stated rather than attaching it to specific people.

<Pull attribution="Punt Road Wines, on their history page">

</Pull>

All fruit is drawn from the estate rather than bought in, and the producer describes its growing as practising organic methods, with certification held through Sustainable Winegrowing Australia. That body runs a sustainability program rather than one of the recognised organic certifiers such as ACO, NASAA or Demeter, which is why this entry records the practice as ongoing rather than as a formal organic certification. No winemaking techniques such as wild fermentation, unfined or unfiltered bottling, or minimal sulphur additions are stated on the producer's page, so none are claimed here.

The cellar door is described as a new tasting facility built at the centre of the estate, featuring a barrel hall and cast iron chandeliers, and sitting beside the Meletos and The Farmhouse accommodation on the property. Tastings are described as seated and guided by staff, though the record gives no hours, booking requirements or fees, so those details are left out rather than assumed.

Commercial access to the wines is offered through a membership program called the Punters Club, which the producer describes as offering a choice of three, six or twelve bottles per shipment, free shipping, and a discount of up to 25 per cent off wine prices. No separate cellar door pricing or online shop link is stated, so this entry does not claim a retail shop or national shipping capability beyond what the membership terms describe.

No specific grape varieties, vessel types or wine styles are named in the record, and no production volume is published, so those fields are left unstated rather than inferred from the two ranges the producer names.
