Magpie Springs operates from Brookman Road on Willunga Hill, in country the record places across both the Adelaide Hills and Fleurieu Peninsula regions. It is an estate winery: the fruit is grown on the property itself, and no parent company mentions, shared address, or contradictory ownership signal appears in the record, so independence is recorded as clear. No founding year is published on the site.

The vineyard is described in the producer's own material as historic, and the winery states that its wines are nurtured organically on site, though no certifying body is named anywhere in the record. Under the standing convention used here, a stated but uncertified organic practice is classified as "practising" rather than "certified".

Winemaking is carried out on the property, according to the record's winemaking facts, though no specific varieties or finished wine styles are named on the page.

The cellar door presents itself as a combined offering: cellar door, art gallery, and vineyard experience, set among gardens and what the site calls rustic, character-filled buildings, with a pergola area. General cellar door hours are Saturdays and Sundays, 11am to 5pm, extended to public holidays, by appointment, and through an extended summer season. A separate Thursday opening, branded Plein Air, runs 11am to 4pm at $10 per person.

A pre-booked Wine Tasting Experience runs between 11am and 3pm. It includes a full tasting, a glass of the visitor's preferred wine, and a platter prepared for two, priced at $75 for two people or $150 for four. Bookings are described as essential and are arranged by phone. Wines are stated to be available exclusively at the cellar door, alongside an on-site Magpie Springs Wine Bottle Shop; no online retail channel is named in the record.

The property also operates as an event venue on a dry-hire basis, able to accommodate more than 250 guests in the gardens surrounded by vineyard. Hirers may bring their own styling and catering, though the property requires that Magpie Springs wines be served at any event held there. Outdoor bookings are described as weather dependent, with marquees available if needed, and prospective hirers are asked to book a site visit by phone.

Adelaide artist Avril Thomas is named as the resident artist, with work exhibited on site and described as a finalist in competitions including the Archibald Prize, along with wins in international art competitions and placement in corporate and private collections in Australia, Hong Kong and the United States. The producer separately runs Paint and Sip classes in partnership with Creative Beginnings, and sells art, gifts and homewares through an online shop hosted on Redbubble.
