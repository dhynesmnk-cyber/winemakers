Art Wine works from two estate vineyards in two different regions. The home block sits on Bird in Hand Rd at Woodside, in the Adelaide Hills, and the second, named Springfarm, is in the Clare Valley. Both are described as estate grown and single vineyard sources, and the producer states that it makes more than 17 different wines between them.

The site describes the business as a family-run winery, though no family members are named.

That statement is enough to flag the business as independently run, but not enough to confirm the ownership structure without a names-based check against the company register, which is why the record treats independence as pending rather than settled.

On winemaking, the record lists minimal sulphite use as a stated practice. Wild ferment, unfined and unfiltered are not claimed anywhere on the site, so they are left out here rather than assumed. No organic or biodynamic certification is stated for either vineyard, and no certifying body is named for either practice, which puts Art Wine in the same position as many small estate producers: minimal intervention is described, but nothing is certified.

No specific grape varieties appear on the site. The producer mentions a focus on Mediterranean varieties and states that the range has been awarded 42 trophies, but without a named variety or a named award, neither can be listed with any specificity here.

History on record is thin. The site notes that the vineyards were planted "many years after" the winery's first vines went in, without giving a founding date or a planting year.

The cellar door is open daily. The record describes seated, guided tastings accompanied by a cheese platter, alongside a more casual option: visiting as a wine bar for a glass or a bottle. Small plates and seasonal local food platters are available, and the site lists gluten free, vegetarian and vegan options. The cellar door itself is described as architecturally designed and fully accessible, set among vines with Adelaide Hills views.

A wine club is mentioned as offering delivery direct to the door. No online shop, price list or tasting fee appears in the record, so none of those details are given here. Pricing and any purchase mechanism should be confirmed directly with the producer.
