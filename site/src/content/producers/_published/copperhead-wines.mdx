Copperhead Wines is a small estate winery on St Huberts Rd at Yering, in the Yarra Valley. The producer describes the site as sitting in a part of the region with a high diurnal temperature range, which it says slows ripening and increases fruit flavour, though this is the producer's own account of the site rather than an independent assessment.

The business is associated with Hans and Anna Orth, who moved from a wholesale plant nursery into grape growing and eventually into wine. The producer describes their partnership as spanning 40 years, with over 30 years of experience between them. The record does not state when the vineyard was planted or when the winery itself began operating, so no founding year is given here.

The vineyard grows Chardonnay and Shiraz. The record cites a 2020 Chardonnay and a 2017 Shiraz by name, though it does not say whether all fruit is grown on the property or whether some is bought in, so no fruit-source claim is made here.

Tastings run by appointment only, Monday to Friday, from 10am to 4:30pm, and take place in the wine production area rather than a dedicated cellar door. The producer notes that a cellar door is coming soon, which the record treats as a stated fact about the property's current state rather than something to infer. Visitors taking a tasting are also walked through the gardens on the property as part of the experience. There is no restaurant or café on site. Pricing is not itemised in the record beyond the producer's own description of it as "extremely reasonable," and no specific tasting fee is stated.

Beyond the property, Copperhead has taken part in industry events around Victoria, including exhibiting at the Wine & Vine Festival in Melbourne and at the Melbourne Good Food & Wine Show, and joining a multi-winery event in Healesville alongside 28 other Yarra Valley producers.

No organic or biodynamic certification or practice is claimed in the record, and none of wild ferment, unfined, unfiltered or minimal-SO2 handling is documented, so all are recorded here as not evidenced rather than assumed absent for other reasons. Ownership signals show no parent company mentions, no shared address and no shared contact domain with another business, which is consistent with the clear independence the record assigns to the winery. The people named in connection with the property are Hans Orth, Anna Orth, a staff member named Amy, and a resident dog named Luigi, though the record gives no further detail on their roles.
