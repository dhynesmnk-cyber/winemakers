Silver Lining Wines is an estate winemaker in the Adelaide Hills, South Australia. The record gives no physical address, suburb or founding year, only that the label was created by Marty Edwards after 19 years working at The Lane Vineyard, where he learned the trade from his father. In 2012 Edwards was diagnosed with Parkinson's Disease; the winery was established afterward, and the producer states that sale proceeds contribute to Parkinson's Disease Research in Australia.

No percentage or mechanism for that contribution is given, so the extent of the arrangement is not something this entry can state beyond the producer's own words.

The fruit is drawn entirely from estate vineyards, spread across four sites in the Adelaide Hills. The Sauvignon Blanc block was planted in 2001 with the F4V6 clone at 2666 vines per hectare, described by the producer as sitting in a "mini" valley just east of Hahndorf with a chilly microclimate.

The Chardonnay was planted in 2004 with the B95 clone, also at 2666 vines per hectare, on a site just outside Macclesfield described as hilly, with free-draining sandy loam over ironstone topsoils. Pinot Noir is sourced from one of the steepest sites in the central hills, outside Lobethal. The Shiraz is a blend of two parcels: one on a sun-filled aspect overlooking the Mt Bold Reservoir toward the southern boundary, and one planted in 1998 with the 1654 clone at 2666 vines per hectare, on the opposite side of the Chardonnay property near Macclesfield.

Winemaking detail in the record is limited to two vintages. The 2023 Chardonnay is described as having seen finely integrated new and seasoned French oak, and the 2023 Pinot Noir also saw French oak. No fermentation or handling practices, such as wild yeast, fining, filtration or minimal sulphur use, are mentioned for any of the wines, and no vintages beyond 2023 are described.
