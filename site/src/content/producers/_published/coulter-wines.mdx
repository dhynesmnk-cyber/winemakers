Coulter Wines is associated with the Adelaide Hills region of South Australia. No street address, suburb or cellar door details are published on the site, and the record gives no basis for locating the operation more precisely.

Several named vineyard sites appear across the range. One Tree Hill Vineyard is described as having clay soils over a limestone base at an elevation of 250 metres. A single-vineyard site called Rathmine sits in Echunga and supplies the Chardonnay. The Pinot Noir is drawn from two cooler single-vineyard sites, at Ironbank and Kenton Valley. Beyond these named sources, the record does not state whether all fruit is estate-grown or whether some is bought in, which is why fruit source here is recorded as mixed rather than estate.

The range covers Sangiovese, Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Barbera and a Sangiovese-Cabernet Sauvignon blend. The Sangiovese wines are made with carbonic maceration and a small proportion of whole-bunch ferment: five per cent for the 2024 Adelaide Sangiovese and ten per cent for the C2 Sangiovese. The Chardonnay is barrel fermented. Bottle numbers are stated for some releases, 2000 bottles for the 2024 Adelaide Sangiovese and 2300 for the 2024 Barbera, though the Pinot Noir figure is recorded inconsistently in the source, listed as both 600 and 6000 bottles, and that contradiction is not resolved here.

A separate label, Perihelion, is described as an export project directed at the United States market. It uses lightweight bottles, labels made from 100% recycled sugar cane, and capsules and cartons made with 40% recycled materials. Fruit for its Chardonnay is stated to be grown using sustainable practices, though no certifying body is named for this claim. How Perihelion relates to Coulter Wines as a business is not detailed beyond the export framing.

Independent reviews are recorded for several wines. The 2023 C2 Sangiovese scored 95 points from Halliday Wine Companion and the label has been placed in the Halliday Top 100 in successive years. The 2024 Chardonnay scored 92+ points from Campbell Mattinson and 92 from Tom Kline. The 2024 Pinot Noir scored 92 points from both Campbell Mattinson and Mike Bennie.

Sales appear to run directly from the producer rather than through a cellar door. Wine club pricing is listed for most releases: A$24.00 for the Sangiovese wines, A$29.60 for the Pinot Noir and Barbera, and A$40.00 for the 2021 Sangiovese Cabernet Sauvignon blend. A mixed six-pack of C2 Sangiovese, three bottles each of the 2022 and 2023 vintages, is listed at $190 including postage. Free freight is offered within Australia for orders of twelve bottles or more.

No hours, ownership statements or founding details appear on the site. The record notes that there is no parent company mention, no ABN and no ownership statement to draw on, which leaves independence unconfirmed rather than established either way.
