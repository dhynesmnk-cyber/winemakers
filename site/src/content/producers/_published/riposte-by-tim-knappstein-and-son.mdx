Riposte by Tim Knappstein and Son is based in the Adelaide Hills of South Australia. No street address, suburb or founding year is stated in the record, and no coordinates are published on the site.

The producer describes itself as "wholly family owned and run," and names Tim Knappstein as winemaker and owner. A second statement in the record refers to "Tim Knappstein and son, family operation," though the son is not named and no further detail on the family structure is given. That is enough to describe the business as family-run, but not enough to attach names beyond Tim Knappstein himself.

The record states that Tim Knappstein has more than fifty years of experience in winemaking and describes him as a recognised pioneer of the Adelaide Hills region. No vintage, vineyard or award detail accompanies that claim, so it is reported here as the producer's own statement about its winemaker rather than as an independently verified fact.

Only one variety appears in the record: Pinot. The Harvester's own confidence notes flag that this is the sole variety explicitly listed on the page, without further specification of Pinot Noir, Pinot Gris or Pinot Meunier. Whether the fruit is grown on an estate vineyard or purchased from growers elsewhere in the region is also not stated, so no fruit source can be recorded.

Nothing in the record describes the winemaking process. Fermentation method, filtration, fining and sulfur use are all left unaddressed, and the record's practice fields are accordingly all set to not stated. No organic or biodynamic claim, certified or practising, is made anywhere in the source material.

The site's tastings, pricing and hours sections are empty in the record, so cellar door status cannot be confirmed one way or the other. The single commercial detail available is that the winery offers free shipping within Australia on full case purchases, which points to an online sales channel operating through the main website, though no separate shop address is given.

Given how little public detail the page provides beyond ownership and winemaker background, this entry stays short. What the record supports is a family-run South Australian winery in the Adelaide Hills, led by a long-serving winemaker, working principally with the variety it lists simply as Pinot.
