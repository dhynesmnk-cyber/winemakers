Howard Vineyard operates from 53 Bald Hills Rd, Nairne, in the Adelaide Hills, a site the producer's own page places 25 minutes from the Adelaide CBD. A cellar door and a restaurant run from the same address. The business describes itself as a "second generation family owned business", though the record names no one in that family and gives no founding year, so the claim stands as a producer statement rather than a documented fact.
