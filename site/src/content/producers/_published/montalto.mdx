Montalto is an estate winery on the Mornington Peninsula. The producer's own site describes the vineyards as sitting in a cool-climate, coastal location, and the record's fruit source is listed as estate throughout, with no mention of bought-in grapes. No street address, suburb, or founding year is given anywhere in the record, and there is no ownership statement to confirm who holds the business, so independence here is noted as unconfirmed rather than assumed.

The vineyards are managed by Dan Prior. Winemaking is led by Simon Black. Three labels are produced from the estate's plantings, and the winemaking is said to be shaped around qualities the producer attributes to the individual vineyard sites. No grape varieties are named in the record, despite this discussion of winemaking approach, and no vessel types, wine styles, or production volumes are stated either.

A 1km trail runs through the vineyards along wooden boardwalks and through wetlands, and is described as taking up to an hour on foot. This sits alongside two dining operations rather than a conventional tasting room: The Restaurant at Montalto, which runs a set menu built on estate-grown heirloom produce and locally sourced ingredients, and The Piazza, a casual option overlooking the vines and the sculpture installations. Julie Bennett is named as Produce Manager, with responsibility for the estate gardens that appear to feed this produce back into the restaurants, though the record does not spell out that connection explicitly.
