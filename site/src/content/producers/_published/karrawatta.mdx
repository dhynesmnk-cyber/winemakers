Karrawatta is a South Australian producer drawing fruit from three family-owned vineyards spread across Adelaide Hills, McLaren Vale, and Langhorne Creek. No street address, suburb, or cellar door hours appear on the producer's about-us page, so this entry cannot say whether the property is open to visitors, and none of the usual visiting detail, a booking line, an opening day, a tasting fee, is published anywhere in the record.

The name carries a specific family history. Joseph Gilbert, described on the page as Mark Gilbert's great, great, great-grandfather, made his first wine in 1847 on a property then called Karrawatta. The producer's own history states that the property was later renamed Pewsey Vale, the result of a lost bet at a local South Australian pub, and that the Karrawatta name has since been revived for the current wine label. Mark and Ali Gilbert are named as the current owners, which is enough to support calling this a family-owned operation rather than the vaguer "family business" language some producers use without naming anyone.
