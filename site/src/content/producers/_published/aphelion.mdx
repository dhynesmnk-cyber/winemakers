Aphelion is a McLaren Vale producer founded in 2014 as what its own site calls a "Grenache project." The range has since grown to include Mourvèdre, Grenache Blanc and Chenin Blanc, but the producer is explicit that Grenache remains the centre of the operation rather than one variety among several.

<Pull attribution="Aphelion, on their website">
  Aphelion began with Grenache. Our focus is Grenache. Our future is Grenache.
</Pull>

No street address, suburb or set opening hours for a cellar door appear on the site. The wines are built from named single vineyards across McLaren Vale. The Affinity blend takes Grenache from a Seaview sub-district vineyard owned by the Romano family and Mourvèdre from a Willunga South vineyard owned by the Lacey family. The Brini Single Vineyard Grenache and Chenin Blanc come from the Brini family's holdings in Blewitt Springs, the Chenin Blanc from a house block first bottled by Aphelion in 2019. The Confluence blend combines Clarendon fruit from the Hickinbotham vineyard with Blewitt Springs parcels from the Brini and Wait families. The Emergent Mourvèdre comes from a roughly three-acre Lacey family block in White's Valley, hand-picked. The Callow Grenache Blanc is sourced from the Yangarra vineyard near Kangarilla, which the producer states is certified organic and biodynamic; the certifying body is not named on the page.

Winemaking across the range leans on whole-bunch and partial whole-bunch fermentation, ageing in neutral French oak alongside concrete and stainless steel, and bottling without fining or filtration. The Confluence ferments partly in a 1,500-litre cocciopesto vessel and a 950-litre concrete egg before finishing in French foudre and demi-muid oak. Sulphur additions are kept low across the wines, and every wine detailed on the site is described as vegan. Stated alcohol levels run from 13.2% to 14.2%, and production is small: the Brini Grenache and Chenin Blanc are bottled in runs of 800 and 600 bottles, the Emergent Mourvèdre in 85 dozen, and the Callow Grenache Blanc limited to 250 six-packs.

Rob Mack is named as winemaker, and the site describes him as making the selections for the wine club. Membership terms include a minimum of one year and twelve bottles, discounts of 5, 10 or 15 per cent on core-range RRP, free shipping, pre-release access, and no exit fees once the minimum term is served. Cellar door tastings are described as available and free for club members and their guests, with no published address or hours for the general public.

Pricing on the site runs from $28 for the Welkin Grenache to $150 for the Rapture, with members' pricing quoted separately for some single-vineyard bottlings. Several wines carry critic scores from Halliday Wine Companion, Decanter, The Wine Front, Robert Parker Wine Advocate and The Real Review, cited on the producer's own pages.

Ownership beyond Rob Mack's role as winemaker is not stated on the site, and no ABN or shared corporate address was found in the record, which is why independence is recorded here as a status to check rather than confirmed.
