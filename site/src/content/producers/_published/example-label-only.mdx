Example Label Only buys fruit from growers in the Yarra Valley and has done
since 2019. There is no vineyard and no winery building open to visitors. The
wine is made under a shared-space arrangement and sold through the producer's
own shop.

The producer publishes no address, which is the ordinary arrangement for a label
at this scale rather than an omission. A reader looking for somewhere to visit
will not find one here, and this entry does not invent one: there is no cellar
door, no set of opening hours and no map pin, because the producer has published
none of those things.

Two wines are made, a chardonnay and a pinot noir, both from purchased fruit.
Purchased fruit is recorded here as a fact about how the business works and not
as a mark against it. Production sits under a thousand cases.

<Pull attribution="Example Label Only, on their about page">
  We do not own a vineyard and we have never claimed to. We buy from four
  growers and we name them on every back label.
</Pull>

Fermentation is spontaneous. The wines go into stainless steel and old
barriques. Nothing here describes how either wine tastes, and nobody working on
this directory has opened a bottle of it: what is recorded is what the producer
has published about their own operation, with the page it came from cited at the
foot of this entry.
