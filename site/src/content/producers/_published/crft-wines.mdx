CRFT Wines is based at Carey Gully in the Adelaide Hills, where the estate vineyard, Arranmore, sits on Peramangk Country in the Piccadilly Valley. The winery was founded in 2012 by winemakers Candice Helbig and Frewin Ries, who purchased Arranmore in 2016 and began converting it to organic farming that same year. The vineyard has held NASAA Certified Organic status since February 2019.

Helbig grew up in the Barossa Valley and began working in wine at seventeen. She studied Wine Science through Charles Sturt University and has completed more than twenty vintages, including time at Hardy Wine Company and Mollydooker. Ries grew up on New Zealand's North Island and completed his first vintage at Cloudy Bay in Marlborough, also at seventeen, before graduating with a wine diploma at nineteen. He has worked more than twenty vintages across upward of fifteen wineries and vineyards in New Zealand, Australia, the United States and France, including a stretch in Marlborough between 1998 and 2001 that shaped his interest in single-vineyard Pinot Noir, and a 2003 vintage at Williams Selyem in California's Russian River Valley. Helbig and Ries met in 2005 and married in 2014. In 2009 they spent seven months travelling through wine regions in Europe, the United States and South America; a stop in Austria's Wachau region is credited with cementing their attachment to Grüner Veltliner, a variety the record notes was then uncommon in the Adelaide Hills.

The estate range made from Arranmore fruit covers Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Grüner Veltliner. CRFT also makes a Shiraz and a Mencia from Barossa Valley fruit, and sources additional grapes from ten vineyards spread across the Adelaide Hills and Barossa Valley. Each wine is made from grapes picked from a single named vineyard, rather than blended across sites.

Helbig and Ries live at Arranmore with their son and two labradors, sharing the property with the vines they farm. Ries also founded a separate label, Complexity Wines, in 2010, working with family-owned vineyards across South Australia's wine regions; the page does not state whether that label remains active or how it relates to CRFT today.
