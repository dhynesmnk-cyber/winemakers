Watkins is a South Australian producer drawing fruit from family-owned vineyards in the Adelaide Hills and Langhorne Creek. The Watkins branded wine portfolio launched in 2019, when siblings Ben, Sam and Jo took over the family's wine business from their parents. The vineyard history behind the label is older: David and Ros Watkins purchased land and planted a vineyard in 1997.

<Pull attribution="Watkins, on its about page">
  The Watkins family have 27 years of experience in the South Australian wine industry since David and Ros Watkins purchased land in 1997 and planted a vineyard
</Pull>

Before the Watkins label existed, the family operated Fleurieu Vintners out of the Boar's Rock Winery in McLaren Vale, producing bulk wine for other brands. The shift to a family-branded portfolio in 2019 marked a change from that bulk trade to wine sold under the family's own name.

Sam Watkins is the family's first winemaker and holds the Head Winemaker title at Watkins. His training includes vintages under Fiona Donald at Seppeltsfield in the Barossa Valley, Philip Shaw at his eponymous Orange winery, and Sue Hodder at Wynns in Coonawarra, along with vintages at Quinta da Roeda in the Douro Valley and St Supery in Napa. He holds a WSET Level 4 Diploma, having been part of the inaugural South Australian cohort invited into the programme, and a WSET Level 3 certification from 2017. He also completed the Australian Wine and Alcohol Advanced Certificate after receiving the Wayne Thomas Scholarship from McLaren Vale. Separately from the Watkins operation, the record states that Sam has served as Head Winemaker at the largest winery in the Adelaide Hills region for the past seventeen years, and has volunteered with the Langhorne Creek Wine Region for nine years.

The rest of the family holds distinct roles in the business. Jo Watkins is Export, Sales and Marketing Director, having previously worked in politics and government. Ben Watkins is a Director and qualified chartered accountant, with prior consulting experience at PwC and EY. David and Ros Watkins are named as co-founders. The producer states that the wider operation is supported by winemaking, viticulture and administrative teams comprising more than twenty people.
