Dominique Portet is an estate winery at Coldstream, in the Yarra Valley. The producer's own history states it was founded in 2000 by Dominique Portet, whose family winemaking history in France goes back generations; the page describes "centuries of French winemaking heritage" being brought to Australia, though that heritage refers to the family's history overseas rather than to the age of the Australian operation itself, which is twenty-six years old. His son, Ben Portet, is named as continuing the winemaking.

</Pull>

The estate draws on two vineyard sites. The Coldstream vineyard sits beside the winery, planted in 2002 to Cabernet Sauvignon and other Bordeaux varieties in grey loam and alluvial soils. A second block at Gladysdale, established in 2020, lies further east at around 300 metres on deep red volcanic soils. The record states fruit source as estate, and both sites are described as handpicked and carefully managed, with all wines made by hand in small batches. No wild ferment, unfined, unfiltered or minimal-SO2 practices are stated for the estate, and no organic or biodynamic certification is mentioned.

Wines are grouped under named ranges rather than sold as single labels: the Fontaine range sits alongside Single Vineyard wines, a Réserve de Famille tier, and a wine called André. The varieties named in the record are Cabernet Sauvignon, other Bordeaux varieties, Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, which points to a Cabernet-led red program alongside cool-climate Chardonnay and Pinot Noir from the estate's two sites.

The cellar door at Coldstream offers tastings, with private tastings also available; no fee or booking requirement is stated for either. The Bistro on site, open Thursday to Sunday, serves seasonal French-inspired dishes designed to complement the wines, with seating indoors and out and views across the vines. A pétanque pitch is part of the grounds. The producer hosts annual events including Mother's Day, Father's Day and Bastille Day gatherings, vintage launches, and food events with visiting chefs, and offers private bookings ranging from small family lunches to larger corporate functions.
