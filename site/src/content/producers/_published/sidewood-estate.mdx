Sidewood Estate is based in the Adelaide Hills of South Australia, with vineyards spread across four sites at Oakbank, Charleston, Echunga and Nairne. The producer states that these blocks total around 110 hectares, giving the winery an estate-grown supply of fruit rather than one built on outside growers. The business traces its founding to 2004, when Owen and Cassandra Inglis established it.

The company describes itself in those terms, and since the founders are named on the same page, the description here uses family-owned rather than a vaguer family-run. Nothing on the page points to a subsequent sale or a parent company, but the record notes there is no positive statement confirming that the family retains ownership today, so that detail is held under review rather than treated as settled.

Winemaking is led by Dylan Lee, named on the site as the winemaker. The producer describes the winery as having cutting-edge facilities. No detail is given on ferment method, fining, filtration or sulphur handling beyond the absence of any stated wild ferment, unfined, unfiltered or minimal-intervention practice, so none of those descriptors are applied here.

On viticulture, the producer states its fruit is farmed organically. No certifying body is named, and the biodynamic determination is none. Separately, the site claims recognition as the region's largest certified sustainable producer and states it holds Winegrowing Australia accreditation. No certifying body is named for the sustainability claim either. The gap between the certified language used and the absence of a named certifier is left unresolved by the source page.

James Halliday's guide lists Sidewood among its five-red-star wineries, a rating the producer cites directly. The record gives no detail on which vintages or wines earned it.
