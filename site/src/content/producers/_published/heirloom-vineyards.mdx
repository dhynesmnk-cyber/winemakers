Heirloom Vineyards draws fruit from two estate vineyards recorded on its own site: one in the Adelaide Hills, at Birdwood, and one in McLaren Vale. The producer refers to these as "our best Adelaide Hills Vineyard" and "our best McLaren Vale Vineyard" in describing where the fruit for its wines comes from.

The record lists six varieties across the two sites: Pinot Grigio, Sauvignon Blanc, Grenache, Touriga, Tempranillo and Chardonnay. Two wines named in the pricing list are blends rather than single varieties. The Anevo Fortress is recorded as 60% Grenache, 25% Touriga and 15% Tempranillo. The Alcala is recorded as a McLaren Vale Grenache. A separate wine, Assen's Fortalice, is listed as a Chardonnay. Pricing for the full range runs from $30 for the Pinot Grigio and Sauvignon Blanc up to $80 for the Anevo Fortress blend and the Alcala Grenache, with the Tempranillo at $40 and the Chardonnay at $60.

The record notes that an external publication commented on the 2022 vintage of the McLaren Vale Grenache. No detail of that commentary, and no tasting note of any kind, appears in the material the Harvester collected, and none is repeated here.

No address, suburb, opening hours or contact details appear on the page the record was drawn from, and no cellar door information is available. The producer's independence has not been confirmed: the record finds no parent company mentions, no shared address or contact domain, and no explicit ownership statement, so the ownership determination is marked as requiring a check rather than settled. This entry does not assert independence beyond what the source supports.

No organic or biodynamic certification is stated, and no farming practices such as wild ferment, unfined, unfiltered or minimal sulphur are recorded for either vineyard. No production volume, founding year, or names of owners or winemakers appear in the source material, so none are given here.

What the record does establish is a two-region operation built on estate fruit, with a small, named range of white, red and blended wines priced consistently across the list, and a producer that is explicit about which vineyards its fruit comes from even where it says little else about itself.
