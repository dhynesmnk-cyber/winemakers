Vigna Bottin operates in McLaren Vale, South Australia, where the Bottin family has been growing grapes since 1970. The family's history with grape growing goes back further than the Australian vineyard: the producer states that the family grew grapes in Italy for generations before establishing the McLaren Vale site.

<Pull attribution="Vigna Bottin, on their history">
  Our family have been growing grapes in the McLaren Vale wine region since 1970 and in Italy for generations before that.
</Pull>

The estate grows Italian grape varieties, though the record does not name which ones are planted. Winemaking is described as using organic principles with minimal intervention. The producer's organic status is recorded here as practising rather than certified: no certifying body is named on the site, which is the ordinary arrangement for a property of this kind, since formal certification is an ongoing cost that many family growers choose not to carry when the fruit is already farmed to a similar standard without it.

Cellar door visits centre on a seated wine flight, with a choice of six wines from the Casa range or five from the Cantina range, each served with a tasting plate. A booking is required for the tasting, though the record does not publish set hours of operation, so the cellar door is treated here as by appointment rather than open. On-site dining is available alongside the tasting, with a menu designed by Chef Angelo and built from Italian family tradition. The producer describes the atmosphere as homely and welcoming, a description that sits alongside a wine club structure built on family custom rather than on a conventional loyalty scheme.

<Pull attribution="Vigna Bottin, on their wine club">
  Vigna Bottin Wine Clubs, in true Italian tradition, are named after our fathers.
</Pull>

Membership in the wine club carries a 10% discount on purchases from the Casa range. Fruit for Vigna Bottin's wines is estate-grown, and the record names no organic or biodynamic certifier. Practices such as wild fermentation, unfined or unfiltered bottling, and minimal sulphur additions are not stated on the site, so none of them are attributed here.

No street address, suburb, pricing detail or opening hours are published. The founding year given, 1970, is drawn from the family's own statement about when grape growing began in McLaren Vale, rather than from a separately stated founding date for the winery as a business. What the record does support is a family-run estate in McLaren Vale with an Italian focus running through the vineyard, the wine list and the food served alongside it.
