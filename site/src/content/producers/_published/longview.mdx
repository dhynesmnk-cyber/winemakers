Longview is an estate winery at Macclesfield, in the Adelaide Hills region of South Australia. The producer's site states the business was established in 1995, which is the only founding detail on record: no names, no account of who started it or why.

The site describes the business as a family-owned estate, without naming the family involved.

</Pull>

That is a claim worth reporting as a claim rather than a fact the directory can confirm. A family-owned business that names no family is not the same as a business whose ownership has been checked, and this entry treats the phrase accordingly: as something Longview says about itself.

A cellar door is available, according to the site, though no opening hours are published. Alongside it, the property carries a day spa, accommodation and wedding and event spaces. None of these are detailed further on the page, so this entry does no more than list them.

On wine, the record names one variety: Pinot Noir, tied to a wine listed on the site as the '25 Twin Palms Pinot Noir. The page carries flavour and aroma language about that wine, which is the producer's own marketing copy and is not repeated here, since nobody compiling this entry has tasted it. Beyond the variety name, there is no detail on where the fruit is grown, whether it is estate-grown or bought in, or how the wine is made. The record does not support wild ferment, unfined, unfiltered or minimal-SO2 practices, and none are claimed. No organic or biodynamic certification is claimed either, and no certifier is named for either.

Pricing information on the site relates to a loyalty scheme rather than a tasting fee: club members earn points per dollar spent, with a higher rate for Vista Wine Club members, and points can be redeemed against future purchases. No cellar door tasting fee is stated, and no minimum spend or age requirement appears in the record.

The site notes that the property stands on Permamangk Land. That is reported here as the kind of detail that belongs in a factual record of place, alongside the address and the region, rather than as scene-setting.

There is no information in the record on production volume, on whether the wine is sold online, or on whether it ships beyond the cellar door, so those fields are left as unknown rather than guessed. What can be said of Longview, on the evidence available, is limited: an Adelaide Hills estate at Macclesfield, trading since 1995, with a cellar door, a day spa, accommodation and event facilities, and a single named variety on the label.
