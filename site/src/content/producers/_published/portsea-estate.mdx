Portsea Estate occupies the southern tip of Victoria's Mornington Peninsula, on land overlooking Bass Strait. The producer draws fruit from two named estate vineyards. The Portsea Vineyard is described on the site as shaped by ancient limestone deposits, calcareous sands and humus-rich topsoil formed over thousands of years. The Main Ridge Vineyard is planted on ancient basalt-derived soils with underlying clay, a different geology from the home block and one that aligns with the wider Main Ridge subregion of the Peninsula. No hectares, planting years or clonal detail are stated for either site.

The producer describes the property as a family farm of long standing.
