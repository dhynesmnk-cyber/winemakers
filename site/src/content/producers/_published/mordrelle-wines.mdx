Mordrelle Wines is based in Hahndorf, in the Adelaide Hills, and states it was established in 2010. The record gives no street address, only the suburb, and no figures for annual production or vineyard size are published. The producer's own description is "family owned boutique wine producer," and the people named in connection with the business are winemaker Martin Moran, Michelle Moran, David Dreckow and Jane Dreckow, alongside Jose Luis Moran, described as Martin Moran's father and the artist behind the label artwork.

No fruit source is stated for the Adelaide Hills wines. The record does not say whether the grapes are estate-grown or bought in, and the Harvester's own notes flag this as unresolved, so no claim is made here either way. Nothing in the record indicates organic or biodynamic farming, and no certification is named.

The range is broad for a producer of this description. It includes a bottle-fermented Blanc de Blancs, disgorged on demand, alongside Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Noir, Syrah, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Malbec, Barbera, Tempranillo and a Rosé. Apple ciders are also listed among the products, sitting outside the wine range proper. No winemaking detail beyond the disgorgement note is given: nothing on ferment, fining, filtration or sulphur is stated, and the record's practice flags are all set to false accordingly.

Cellar door tastings are offered by booking rather than on a walk-in basis. The producer pairs these with what it calls a "Wine Tasting Experience," served alongside a Traditional Argentinian barbecue cooked over a fire. No hours, no fee and no minimum age are published in the source material, so none are given here. A wine club, called El Club de Vinos, is also offered as a subscription service, though no pricing or terms are stated for it.

The label artwork is credited to Jose Luis Moran, named in the record as the winemaker's father. No other design, packaging or branding detail is given.

Independence is marked as needing further check in the underlying record: the family ownership claim is made by the producer but the individual relationships between the named people are not fully spelled out, and no ABN or ownership register detail is provided. This entry reflects that position rather than resolving it.
