Crittenden Wines operates from Dromana, on the Mornington Peninsula in Victoria. The producer describes the vineyard as set amongst the vines at the base of Red Hill, which places the estate in the hillier country behind the peninsula's coastline rather than on the flats closer to the water.

The setting is built around a lake as much as a vineyard. The record describes a lakeside estate overlooking vineyard and lake, with private lakeside villas available for overnight stays. That combination of cellar door, restaurant and accommodation puts the operation closer to a hospitality estate than a bare production shed, though the record gives no figures on how much of the business each side represents.

Tastings are offered at the cellar door on a guided basis, though no specific hours are published in the record, so no opening times can be given here. Dining is a stated part of the offering: an on-site restaurant provides seasonal dining, and pricing is given for a midweek lunch, two courses for $65 or three for $79, with a glass of Geppetto included. Geppetto appears in the record as a lunch inclusion rather than as a documented grape variety, so it is not listed here as a varietal.

A separate one-night truffle dinner is also stated: five courses built around premium ingredients, paired with Crittenden wines, and featuring stories from Flinders Truffles and Rollo. No date or recurring schedule is given for this event, so it is reported as a stated offering rather than as an annual fixture.

The record includes no information on fruit sourcing, and no note on whether the fruit behind these wines is estate-grown, bought in, or a mixture of both. It also carries no detail on winemaking practices, no mention of organic or biodynamic status, and no vessel or variety information beyond the Geppetto lunch inclusion.
