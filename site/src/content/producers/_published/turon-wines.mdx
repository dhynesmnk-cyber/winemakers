Turon Wines is an estate winery in the Adelaide Hills, with its address and cellar door at Ondeen Farm, 15 Onkaparinga Valley Rd, Verdun SA 5245. The record gives no founding year and no named owners, so neither is stated here.

The Estate Series Pinot Noir is drawn from a single vineyard at Lenswood, planted at high density: 5,556 vines per hectare. The record states the site was chosen over more than a decade of searching.

<Pull attribution="Turon Wines, on the Lenswood vineyard">
  Vineyard site selected over a decade, described as 'Grand Cru' site.
</Pull>

Beyond the Estate Series, the range extends to a Hills Series and a Limited Series. The Hills Series covers a Gamay, a Pinot Noir, a Chardonnay and a Pinot Gris, priced between $28 and $38 a bottle in the record. The Limited Series adds a Chardonnay and a second Gamay at $50 each, and the producer states this tier is hand-selected fruit from other Adelaide Hills vineyards rather than the home block, which sits alongside the estate-grown Pinot Noir as a second, purchased-fruit strand within the one label. The Estate Series Pinot Noir itself is listed at $60. A gift box, "Fizz, White and Bright," is priced at $100 in the record's pricing facts, though the wines that make it up are not specified.

The producer's winemaking notes describe the Estate Series and the Limited Series in terms of care taken rather than method used, naming no specific viticultural or winemaking practice for either. All series are described as "Sustainably certified" and "Vegan friendly." No certifying body is named for the sustainability claim, so it is recorded here as a producer statement rather than a verified certification. The wider descriptors used on the site, Lo-Fi, Sustainable, Vegan and Cool Climate, are noted as stated rather than independently confirmed.

The cellar door at Ondeen Farm is open Monday to Thursday and on weekends from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and on Friday from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM. The record describes the cellar door as offering guided tastings, long lunches and seasonal bites, which points to an on-site food service alongside tastings, though no specific menu, fee or booking requirement is stated.

Ownership signals in the record are limited: no ABN, no shared address or contact domain with another entity, and no parent company mentioned. Independence is recorded as clear on the basis of the estate-grown fruit statement and a single-label presentation, with the caveat that the Limited Series' use of other Adelaide Hills vineyards means fruit sourcing varies across the range even though the winery itself is categorised as an estate winery.
