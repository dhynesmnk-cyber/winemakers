Sinclair's Gully operates from Norton Summit in the Adelaide Hills, on a 20-acre property the producer describes as a private nature sanctuary adjoining Morialta Conservation Park. The site sits on the Heysen and Yurrebilla Trails, about 20 minutes from the Adelaide CBD. No founding year appears in the record.

Sean Delaney is named as winemaker, owner and vigneron. The producer's own copy attributes the winery's approach to "the Delaneys" as a family, though no other family members are named and the record does not set out the ownership structure beyond that reference.

The vineyard is farmed using biodynamic methods, combined with native grasses intended to support beneficial insects, and the producer describes a strong focus on soil health. No biodynamic certifier is named, so the practice is recorded here as stated rather than certified. No organic certification or practice is claimed for the property.

The estate grows Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay and Pinot Noir under Adelaide Hills cool climate conditions, according to the record's determinations. The range also includes a Barossa Shiraz and a McLaren Vale Grenache. The record does not state whether that fruit is estate-grown or bought in, so no assumption is made either way beyond what is on file.

Sparkling wine makes up a substantial part of the output: the producer describes four sparkling wines and states that this is one of the larger sparkling ranges in the Adelaide Hills. Wines are hand disgorged in small batches on site, using what the producer calls old world techniques of hand disgorging, dosage and corking. A visitor experience, the Sparkling Winemaking Session, runs at $95 per person and includes a walkthrough of the disgorging and dosage process, an introduction to the biodynamic vineyard approach, and a bottle of sparkling wine to take home.

The cellar door is open on Sundays from 12pm to 4pm between 1 September and 24 May, and closed over winter from 24 May to 31 August. It also opens for Good Friday and Easter Monday from 12.30pm to 3.30pm for live music and picnics, though it is closed on Easter Saturday and Sunday. Wine can be ordered and picked up by phone seven days a week outside those hours. Picnics are welcome at the cellar door, and the property hosts live music events and a wine club offering pre-release tastings and member events.

No production figures, pricing beyond the sparkling session, or online shop are stated in the record.
