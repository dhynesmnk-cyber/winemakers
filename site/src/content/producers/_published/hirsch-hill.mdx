Hirsch Hill sits in the Yarra Valley, Victoria, with vines first planted in 1998. Eddie Hirsch purchased the property that year, and his brother Jack joined soon after to help develop the venture. The record states that the family recognised the site's viticultural promise the following year, and an inaugural vintage was harvested in 2001.

The business shifted its focus to commercial sales in 2004, placing wine into restaurants and bottle shops across the Yarra Valley and Melbourne's inner suburbs, and its own account describes 2006 as the point at which its reputation for quality solidified. Ben Hirsch moved into the General Manager role in 2020, bringing a background in sales and marketing, and became CEO in 2023. Peter Mackey was appointed Chief Winemaker in 2022, with his first vintage for the company in 2023.

The winery describes itself as family-run, and the Hirsch family are named across founder, ownership and management roles in Eddie, Jack, and Ben Hirsch. No specific award is named anywhere in the record, so the phrase above is reported here as the producer's own description rather than treated as an established fact.

In 2024 Hirsch Hill acquired a historic Yarra Valley winery, formerly operated by Sticks and Greenstone, and converted an old horse stable on the new site into a working winery. That facility now supports Hirsch Hill's own winemaking alongside contract winemaking, bottling and storage services for other producers. The wider business has since been described as the Hirsch Wine Group, reflecting an expansion across several brands, though the record does not specify what those other brands are or how the group's ownership sits in relation to the Hirsch Hill label.
