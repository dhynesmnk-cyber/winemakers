Red Hill Estate is an estate winery on the Mornington Peninsula, established in 1989. The producer's own account places the vineyard in the hills of the peninsula, though no street address or suburb appears on the page this entry draws from.

The estate is presented as a family concern. The source page, titled "Our Family," states that Red Hill Estate is guided by the Fabrizio family and names Frank and Dominic Fabrizio as the people described in the winemaking notes: they are said to walk the vineyard rows, taste the fruit, and call the moment of harvest by instinct as well as by science. The page does not clarify whether Frank and Dominic are father and son, brothers, or otherwise related, and it does not state their current roles in the business. For that reason this entry does not call the estate family-owned outright; what can be reported is that the producer describes itself as a family business.

<Pull attribution="Red Hill Estate, on its Our Family page">
For Frank and Dominic Fabrizio, wine is personal.
</Pull>

Fruit is drawn from the estate's own vineyard and from what the producer calls select cool-climate regions across Australia. That is a mixed sourcing arrangement rather than a single-vineyard operation, and the page gives no figures for the split between estate and bought-in fruit. No grape varieties are named anywhere on the page.

On site there is a cellar door and what the producer describes as a traditional restaurant with a seasonal menu, alongside vineyard views. No hours of operation are published, and no tasting fees or booking requirements are stated, so this entry cannot say whether a visit needs to be arranged in advance.

The page makes no claim to organic or biodynamic farming and names no certifier for either. No winemaking practices such as wild fermentation, unfined or unfiltered bottling, or minimal sulphur additions are mentioned, and no specific award is named, so none of these appear in this entry.

The Harvester found no ABN, business registration, or other verifiable record to confirm the Fabrizio family's connection to Red Hill Estate beyond the statements on the "Our Family" page itself. There is no evidence of a parent company or shared ownership with another business. Independence is recorded here as unconfirmed rather than assumed, on the basis that the page's ownership claims have not been checked against a registry.
