Sherrah draws its fruit from a handful of named vineyard sites across McLaren Vale, with plantings in the Seaview, Tatachilla, Blewitt Springs and McLaren Flat subregions. The Lacey vineyard supplies both a Seaview parcel of 0.11 hectares (188 vines producing around 700kg of fruit) and a Tatachilla block of 1654-clone Shiraz grown on its own roots. Other estate sites named in the record include the Gowers vineyard on the edge of Blewitt Springs, the Willamba vineyard with its 30-year-old Chenin Blanc vines, the Wait vineyard's 60-year-old bush-vine Grenache on deep sand, and the Petrucci vineyard in an ancient river bed east of the township. All fruit is estate-grown.

Winemaking varies by wine but follows a consistent approach. The Bush Vine Grenache is fermented with a portion of whole bunches in small open fermenters and aged in French puncheons. The Fiano and Chenin Blanc wines are split between stainless steel tank and seasoned oak, several made with wild yeast and matured on lees without acid additions or fining. The Reserve Shiraz spends two years in new French oak puncheon before a further minimum two years' cellar ageing prior to release. A Fortified Chenin Blanc, made from a small Blewitt Springs parcel and fortified with grape spirit, sits at the end of the range at 16.9% alcohol.

The record notes that the Bush Vine Grenache from McLaren Flat followed several years of Alex Sherrah working the fruit before its first commercial release, a detail that points to vineyard trial work rather than a formula fixed from the outset.

No physical address, suburb or opening hours are published, and the record gives no founding year or ownership statement beyond naming Alex Sherrah as the person behind the wines. Independence is unconfirmed in the absence of a positive ownership statement. Wines are sold in mixed packs directly through the website: a six-pack of Reserve Shiraz, Bush Vine Grenache and standard Shiraz is offered at a discounted price with free shipping, alongside mixed dozens split between whites and reds.

Marcus Ellis of Halliday Wine Companion has scored several of the wines in the low-to-mid nineties, including 95 points and a place in the 2025 Top 100 Wines for the Fiano, and 95 points for the Bush Vine Grenache from McLaren Flat. The Chenin Blanc, Grenache Rosé, Red et Al. blend and Shiraz from the Lacey Tatachilla site are also named as having received scores in the record, without further detail on when or how those ratings were published.
