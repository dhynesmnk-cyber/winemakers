Mt Lofty Ranges Vineyard is an estate winery in the Adelaide Hills, with vineyards in the Lenswood sub-region at an elevation of 500 metres or more, roughly 35 minutes from the centre of Adelaide. The producer describes itself as family owned, though the website does not name the family involved, so this entry treats that as a producer statement rather than a confirmed fact.

Fruit for the wines described on the site is grown on the estate. The current range includes three chardonnays: an Apple Block Chardonnay, an Aspire Chardonnay fermented with 40% new French oak, and an S&G Chardonnay. The white portfolio also includes a Home Block Riesling and an Aspire Fume Blanc, both from the 2023 vintage, along with a Methode Traditionelle Sparkling from 2020.

The property carries a chef-hatted restaurant with a seasonal menu, and the site describes the winery as boutique. No cellar door hours or tasting fees appear in the material reviewed, so neither is stated here.

The producer lists a run of accolades. Halliday Wine Companion has given the winery a five-star rating for what the site states is its 14th consecutive year, spanning 2013 to 2026. The Methode Traditionelle Sparkling 2020 won a trophy at the Australia & New Zealand Boutique Wine Awards. The Home Block Riesling 2023 took a gold medal and trophy at the Adelaide Hills Wine Show and a further gold medal at the Royal Melbourne Wine Show. The Aspire Fume Blanc 2023 also won gold at the Adelaide Hills Wine Show, where the producer states it was named Best Producer under 100 Tonnes. Great Wine Capitals gave the restaurant a 2024 Award for Excellence in wine tourism, and AGFG lists the restaurant with a 10th consecutive Chef Hat rating for 2025, spanning 2017 to 2026, and a third consecutive two-hat result for 2026.

No founding year, ownership names, farming certifications or online sales information appear in the record, so none are given here. The organic and biodynamic status of the vineyard is not stated as either practising or certified, and none of wild ferment, unfined, unfiltered or minimal sulphur practices are mentioned on the site.
