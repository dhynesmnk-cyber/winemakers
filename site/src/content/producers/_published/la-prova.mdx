La Prova is based in the Adelaide Hills, South Australia, and was founded in 2008 by winemaker Sam Scott, after two decades of work both in South Australia and overseas. Until 2018 Scott ran two labels, Scott Wines and La Prova. From 2019 the focus shifted to La Prova alone.

The producer works with Italian varieties. Prosecco is sourced from King Valley in Victoria and Nero d'Avola from McLaren Vale, alongside fruit drawn largely, though not exclusively, from the Adelaide Hills. The record describes two ranges, La Prova and La Prova Uno, the second built from single-site expressions of what the producer calls exceptional vineyards. No individual vineyard is named, so this entry does not use the term single-vineyard.

Sustainability is described in some detail, without a certification attached to it. The Harvester's record quotes the producer's own page directly:

<Pull attribution="La Prova, on their vineyard page">
All vineyards from which La Prova sources use sustainable vineyard practices
</Pull>

The same page describes the vineyards as pesticide free, with permanent mid-row grasses that remove the need for tilling. Those grasses are said to promote soil health, compete with weeds, absorb spring rain and support beneficial insects. Spray programs are described as target-based, determined by weather conditions rather than a fixed calendar. No organic or biodynamic certifying body is named anywhere in the record, so these practices stand as producer statement, not as certified fact.

The producer also frames its choice of Italian varieties in climate terms, stating that they need less water and thrive in warmer conditions, and describes its overall production philosophy as inherently sustainable. Winemaking itself is described as a matter of vineyard selection and timing: fruit sourced from vineyards judged best suited to each variety, and picking held for what the producer calls the right day. The record also states that all La Prova wines are vegan friendly.

The cellar door is located in the Adelaide Hills, though the record gives no street address. It is open on the first weekend of each month, and no further hours, booking requirement, or fee is stated.

The record gives no figures for annual production, no pricing, and no online shop, so those fields are left blank rather than estimated. Ownership is stated plainly as Sam Scott's, with no parent company or shared ownership signal anywhere in the record.
