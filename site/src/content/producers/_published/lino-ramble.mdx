Lino Ramble is based in McLaren Vale and draws its fruit from vineyards across McLaren Vale and the Adelaide Hills rather than from an estate holding of its own. The history notes on the record state that two friends founded the business, though neither is named on the page. The winemaking notes record a 14th vintage, which places the operation's beginnings some years back, but no founding year is given and the vintage count alone does not establish one.

Two friends and a common thread

The vineyard sources are named in some detail. Fruit is drawn from sites farmed by the Lacey, Quigley and Baldaso families, together with Paxton Viticulture, Chalk Hill Viticulture, and the Adams, Hatwell, Harvey and Smart families. The record groups these growers under the collective title "The Vineyard Wranglers." No individual vineyard is singled out by name, so the fruit is described here as purchased and grower-sourced rather than single-vineyard.

Fruit is carefully selected from single vineyard sites throughout The Vale and Adelaide Hills
</Pull>

On winemaking, the producer states that its wines are hand-made with an emphasis on fruit character, and that the winemaking fingerprint is kept to a minimum so that texture carries the wine. The record's practice checklist lists wild fermentation, unfined, unfiltered and minimal sulphur additions as all unconfirmed, so none of them are claimed here.

Organic farming is described as an inspiration rather than a certified practice. The record cites growers who treat organic farming as a way of looking after the land and buffering against climate change, and the determination sits at "practising" because no certification body is named anywhere on the page. That is an ordinary position for a business built on purchased fruit: certification belongs to whoever farms the vineyard, and a producer buying from several growers across two regions is not in a position to hold a single certificate on their behalf, even where its own approach draws on the idea.

Grape varieties are chosen, according to the vineyard notes, for sustainability, intrigue and drinkability, though no specific varieties are named on the page and none are listed here as a result. Wine styles are likewise not stated.

The label design is credited to Emily Sheppard of Studio E Creative, the only supplier credit outside viticulture that the record carries. No cellar door, opening hours, pricing or online shop details appear in the source material, so none are given here.
